## Market Insights

### Key Trends in Mobile Strategy/Survival Games (2025-2026)

- **4X Strategy Dominance**: Games like Last War, Whiteout Survival dominate top-grossing charts
- **Survival Games Growth**: China leads with top 5 grossing survival titles globally
- **TikTok User Acquisition**: FUNFLY pioneered viral marketing on TikTok
- **Live-Ops Excellence**: Continuous content updates drive retention
- **Cross-Platform Expansion**: Mobile games expanding to PC (e.g., Call of Dragons)
- **Licensed IP Strategy**: Major success with Marvel, Disney, Star Trek brands

### Geographic Distribution

- **China**: Largest mobile game market, dominates survival/strategy genres
- **South Korea**: 3N+K2 conglomerate (Nexon, NCSoft, Netmarble, Smilegate, Kakao, Krafton)
- **Japan**: Strong in RPG and gacha mechanics
- **USA**: Licensed IP and casual strategy leaders
- **Europe**: Strong in simulation and strategy (Finland, Sweden, Germany)
- **Emerging**: Turkey (Dream Games, Peak), Brazil (Wildlife), Singapore (FUNFLY, IGG)

### Revenue Models

- **Free-to-Play + IAP**: Dominant model for strategy/survival games
- **Battle Pass**: Seasonal content monetization
- **Gacha Systems**: Character/hero collection monetization
- **VIP Systems**: Tiered subscription benefits
- **Ad-Supported**: Hyper-casual games (Voodoo model)
